01 / The problem

From answering questions to taking action.

As people delegate tasks to AI, agents need an identity to communicate and a way to pay. Users need control over spending and visibility into what happened.

02 / Product & differentiation

The agent’s everyday account.

An inbox and wallet together connect communication, payments, and confirmations. Set spending limits, track every action, and revoke access anytime.

03 / Why now & opportunity

AI is becoming a channel for completing purchases. McKinsey projects $3–5T in global consumer commerce mediated by agents by 2030.

McKinsey, 2025 ↗

04 / Business model

Subscriptions + usage.

Monthly plans, usage-based MPP pricing, and credit purchases for premium AI quota. The model combines recurring access with spend as agents use the product.

05 / Go-to-market

Start with AI power users.

Reach builders, founders, and operators. Embed through skills and plugins, activate with challenges and bounties, then expand through repeat use and referrals.
